# Enosh Jaques Digital Identity: Brand Guidelines

This document establishes the brand guidelines, tone of voice, writing style, brand personality, and core audience specifications for the digital identity of **Enosh Jaques**.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

- **Primary Brand**: **Enosh Jaques**
  - _Creative Developer, Designer and Founder._
  - Represents a multi-disciplinary creator who designs visual assets, builds code systems, organizes events, and leads creative campaigns.
- **Sub-Brand / Studio**: **Visual Vibe Creation**
  - _An independent creative studio founded by Enosh Jaques in 2022._
  - Focuses on branding, graphic design, visual media, and digital experience design. It is not an incorporated limited company.

---

## 2. Tone of Voice & Writing Style

Our copy must remain:

- **Professional**: Uses clean, formal sentence structures, correct punctuation, and grammatically complete segments.
- **Confident & Ambitious**: Communicates technical and creative capabilities directly. Focuses on achievements (e.g. publishing an Android app, winning student of the year, founding a studio).
- **Honest & Warm**: Always details student status, upcoming university goals, and short-term placement details truthfully.
- **Modern & Direct**: Avoids wordy jargon or fluff. Gets straight to the point.

---

## 3. Core Audience

The website must serve several audiences without becoming confusing:

- **University Admissions**: Reviewing academic history, certifications, subjects, grades, and leadership characteristics.
- **Internship & Placement Recruiters**: Reviewing IT experience, coding capabilities, and professional event organization roles.
- **Freelance & Digital Clients**: Reviewing Visual Vibe Creation capability lists, graphic design mockups, and photography.
- **Developers & Collaborators**: Auditing code quality, Next.js configurations, and GitHub links.

---

## 4. Things We ALWAYS Communicate

- Enosh Jaques is currently a student preparing to start Year 2 BTEC IT in September, before progressing to university.
- Visual Vibe Creation was founded in 2022 by Enosh and handles design, branding, and media.
- FinCalc is fully live on Google Play as Enosh's first published Android application.
- Professional IT exposure was gained through short placements (Asendia, Hadley Property Group, Radisson Blu Goa).

---

## 5. Things We NEVER Claim

- We never claim Visual Vibe Creation is a registered limited company (do not use "Ltd").
- We never claim to have a large agency team, employees, or global corporate offices.
- We never claim to have years of senior engineering experience or master-level full-stack fluency.
- We never invent downloads, ratings, or client reviews for FinCalc or Visual Vibe Creation.
- We never claim university admissions are already confirmed.
