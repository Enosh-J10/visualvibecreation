# Enosh Jaques Digital Identity: Content & Wording Rules

This document outlines the strict guidelines for editing, writing, and displaying information across all pages of the website, enforcing a facts-only rule.

---

## 1. Core Content Rules

- **Never Invent Experience**: Only verified placements (Radisson Blu Goa, Asendia, Hadley Property Group) and self-employment (Visual Vibe Creation) may be listed.
- **Never Exaggerate Dates**: Do not extend dates of placements or experience. Short-term placements (approx. 1 week) must be described as such. Radisson Blu Goa IT exposure must be capped at 2 months.
- **Never Invent Statistics**: Do not show fake download numbers, active users, star ratings, review feedback, or revenue metrics.
- **Never Claim University Admission**: Use wording such as: *"Planning to study Computer Science or Software Engineering"* or *"Next step: University applications in related fields"*.
- **No Vague Qualifications**: Do not list unverified grade conversions as official UK credentials.
- **No Unused Skills**: Do not list programming languages (e.g. Java, Python, C++) unless Enosh has actively used them.
- **No Fake Testimonials**: If there are no verified quotes from past clients, do not show testimonials. Use placeholder blocks labeled *"Future Testimonials"* or omit them entirely.

---

## 2. Terminology Guidelines

- **Public Name**: Use **Enosh Jaques** globally. Use **Enosh Olencio Jaques** only in formal CV documents, legal footers, and structured JSON-LD schemas.
- **Visual Vibe Creation**: Never write "Creations" or "Ltd". VVC is a secondary visual abbreviation only.
- **Main Self-Employment Label**: Always write:
  `Founder and Self-Employed Creative — Visual Vibe Creation`
- **Goan Tiatr**: Always use the spelling "Tiatr", never "Tiart".
- **Short Placements**: Do not list these with exaggerated job titles like "Systems Administrator" or "Full Stack Intern". Use:
  `Short work-experience placement providing exposure to a professional IT environment.`
- **Academic Grades**: Clearly show BTEC IT BTEC Year 2 progression and T Level Foundation **D*D** score. High School distinctions must match **79%** (St. Pius X) and **75%** (Rosary Higher Secondary).
- **FinCalc**: Always show that FinCalc is fully live on Google Play. Do not label it "In Development" or "Coming Soon".
