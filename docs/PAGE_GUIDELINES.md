# Enosh Jaques Design System: Page Design Guidelines

This document details the purpose, target visitor, expected reading time, visual focus, animation level, and content density for every route in the project.

---

## 1. Home Page (`/`)
- **Purpose**: A narrative-driven storytelling flow. Introduces Enosh Jaques, his skills, and visual vibe.
- **Target Visitor**: General recruiters, creative collaborators, prospective clients.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 3 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Hero statement, interactive project showpieces, and the Goa-to-London timeline.
- **Animation Level**: High (Standard text reveals, magnetic buttons, parallax card layouts).
- **Content Density**: Moderate. Summarizes long-form sections with links to subpages.

---

## 2. About Page (`/about`)
- **Purpose**: Deep-dive personal biography detailing interests in design, coding, photography, and video.
- **Target Visitor**: University admissions, team leaders, recruiters.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 5 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: High-quality portrait images, formatted text paragraphs.
- **Animation Level**: Low-Moderate (Simple content fade-ins on scroll).
- **Content Density**: High. Written in a narrative tone within a reading container (`max-w-3xl` / 60-75 chars).

---

## 3. Projects Page (`/projects`)
- **Purpose**: Central hub of all development, app, and web work.
- **Target Visitor**: Developers, tech recruiters, software managers.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 2 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Tech grid cards featuring project covers and tags.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Hover expansion effects).
- **Content Density**: Moderate-High. Grid list mapping technology and link buttons.

---

## 4. FinCalc Case Study Page (`/projects/fincalc`)
- **Purpose**: Showcase the development case study of the FinCalc Android app.
- **Target Visitor**: Technical leads, software developers.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 4 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Android phone screenshot showcase, challenges, lessons learned, and Play Store download buttons.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Smooth slide-in screenshot arrays).
- **Content Density**: High. Focuses on code, Java/Kotlin, UI choices, and launch metrics.

---

## 5. Portfolio Page (`/portfolio`)
- **Purpose**: Gallery of graphic designs, brand packages, poster design, and professional photography.
- **Target Visitor**: Brand directors, media clients, videography leads.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 1.5 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Fluid grid layout of vector design mockups and photography thumbnails.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Image overlay slide-ups on hover).
- **Content Density**: Very High (Visual assets over heavy text descriptions).

---

## 6. Services Page (`/services`)
- **Purpose**: List capabilities of Visual Vibe Creation studio (Branding, Graphic design, UI design, Video editing).
- **Target Visitor**: Small businesses, freelance clients, marketing managers.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 2 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Glassmorphic service capability cards.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Entrance fade-ins).
- **Content Density**: Moderate. Bullet lists mapping design deliverables.

---

## 7. Experience Page (`/experience`)
- **Purpose**: Display professional timeline, internships, self-employment, and Radisson/Asendia/Hadley placements.
- **Target Visitor**: Recruiters, employers.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 2.5 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Refined vertical work experience timeline.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Timeline line trace and node highlights on scroll).
- **Content Density**: Moderate. Bullet points highlighting IT tasks and responsibilities.

---

## 8. Education Page (`/education`)
- **Purpose**: Academic background (distinctions at St. Pius and Rosary, BTEC Level 3 progression, and university plans).
- **Target Visitor**: University admissions, education boards, employers.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 2 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Academic milestone panels, subject lists, and grades (D*D).
- **Animation Level**: Low. (Ensures high readability).
- **Content Density**: High. Structured tables or cards listing schools, dates, and courses.

---

## 9. Leadership Page (`/leadership`)
- **Purpose**: Showcase event organization, esports planning, Student Union handler, and Goan Tiatr theatrical experience.
- **Target Visitor**: Student committees, event organizers, recruiters.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 2 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Event showcases and Tiatr theater performance summaries.
- **Animation Level**: Moderate.
- **Content Density**: Moderate. Bullet lists showing organization scale.

---

## 10. Awards Page (`/awards`)
- **Purpose**: Highlight college achievements and cycling medals.
- **Target Visitor**: General visitors.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 1 minute.
- **Visual Focus**: Clean SVG trophy panels (Student of the Year, Cycling).
- **Animation Level**: Moderate (Subtle vector rotation on hover).
- **Content Density**: Low. Clear descriptions of the awards.

---

## 11. Certifications Page (`/certifications`)
- **Purpose**: Grid displaying verified Cisco Academy certificates and Springpod IT virtual placements.
- **Target Visitor**: Hiring managers, technical recruiters.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 1.5 minutes.
- **Visual Focus**: Professional credential cards with direct credential verifications.
- **Animation Level**: Low-Moderate.
- **Content Density**: High. Grid containing credential name, issuer, and date.

---

## 12. Contact Page (`/contact`)
- **Purpose**: Allow visitors to copy emails, check current availability status, and access verified socials.
- **Target Visitor**: All visitors.
- **Expected Reading Time**: 1 minute.
- **Visual Focus**: Focus is directed to direct mailto CTA button (`hello@visualvibecreation.com`) and copy buttons.
- **Animation Level**: Low.
- **Content Density**: Low. Minimal text, high-clarity touch actions.
