# Content Architecture & Editorial Strategy

This document details the editorial strategy, page-by-page content architecture, copy inventory, and asset requirements for the digital identity of Enosh Jaques and Visual Vibe Creation.

---

## 1. Core Editorial Voice & Copywriting Rules

To present a premium digital identity comparable to Stripe, Linear, or Apple, we follow these core copywriting principles:

* **Confident, Modern, and Human**: Speak directly and cleanly. Avoid corporate clichés and standard "resume speak".
* **Truthful & Authentic**: State achievements exactly. Avoid hollow tech buzzwords (e.g., *innovative*, *cutting-edge*, *passionate*, *visionary*, *disruptive*, *world-class*, *industry-leading*) unless backed by specific evidence.
* **Driven by Story & Growth**: Every experience, school, or project should answer: *What did I learn? How did it change my approach? How did I grow?*
* **Maximum Paragraph Length**: Keep paragraphs between 3 to 5 lines. Utilize whitespace to create a premium, readable layout.
* **Single Question Focus for Every Page**:
  * **Home**: Who am I?
  * **About**: How did I get here?
  * **Projects**: What have I built?
  * **Portfolio**: What can I create?
  * **Journey**: How have I grown?
  * **Contact**: How can we work together?

## 2. Page-by-Page Content Architecture

### 2.1 Home Page
* **Core Question**: Who am I?
* **Purpose**: Establish Enosh Jaques' identity as a Creative Developer, Designer, and Founder.
* **Target Visitor**: University admissions boards, engineering leads, and clients looking for design-minded developers.
* **Primary CTA**: "View My Work" (links to `/projects`)
* **Secondary CTA**: "Get In Touch" (links to `/contact`)
* **Reading Time**: 3 minutes.
* **Status Line**: "Based in London • Founder of Visual Vibe Creation"
* **Section Sequence**:
  1. **Identity Showcase (Hero)**: Introduces the person first. 
  2. **Current Status Block**: Concise statement regarding upcoming BTEC year.
  3. **Selected Work (Featured Project Grid)**: Showcases 2 flagship items.
  4. **FinCalc Highlight**: In-depth look at the Android application, focusing on problem-solving rather than marketing features.
  5. **Visual Vibe Creation Studio Intro**: Positioning the studio as an independent creative outlet.
  6. **Experience & Education Snapshot**: Highlighting key growth periods.
  7. **Photography Preview**: Showcasing natural visual composition skills.
  8. **Contact Prompt**: Clean, minimal invite to collaborate.

### 2.2 About Page
* **Core Question**: How did I get here?
* **Purpose**: Present a narrative autobiography focused on transitions and creative discovery.
* **Primary CTA**: "Download CV (PDF)"
* **Secondary CTA**: "Get in Touch" (links to `/contact`)
* **Reading Time**: 4 minutes.
* **Narrative Chapters**:
  * *Where It Started*: Early visual curiosity and childhood focus on composition and color in Goa.
  * *Finding Creativity*: Performing in traditional Goan Tiatr theater before live audiences of 500+ people, building stage confidence and coordination.
  * *Discovering Technology*: Writing first lines of HTML, linking logical code structures to visual design.
  * *Building My Own Studio*: Starting Visual Vibe Creation as an independent outlet for visual design and editing.
  * *Moving to London*: Navigating a life-changing move to the UK, adapting to a global tech capital.
  * *Looking Ahead*: Preparing for Computer Science at university to build useful, premium products.

### 2.3 Projects Page
* **Core Question**: What have I built?
* **Purpose**: Showcase technical development competency and codebase execution.
* **Groupings**:
  * *Published*: FinCalc (flagship Android application).
  * *Current*: This design system and portfolio framework.
  * *Experimental*: Micro coding tools and script utilities.
  * *Future (Planned)*: Upcoming projects in the concept phase.

### 2.4 Portfolio Page
* **Core Question**: What can I create?
* **Purpose**: Showcase visual design competence across diverse mediums.
* **Categories**:
  * *Graphic Design*: Event layouts and typography.
  * *Brand Identity*: Clean logos and monogram frameworks.
  * *Photography*: Thematic galleries (Architecture, Nature, Street, Events, Portraits).
  * *Videography*: Kinetic animation clips and editing sequences.
  * *UI / Web Design*: High-contrast user interfaces.

### 2.5 Journey Page (Timeline)
* **Core Question**: How have I grown?
* **Purpose**: Document chronological experiences in education, career, and leadership.
* **Factual Timeline Areas**:
  * *Experience*:
    * *Visual Vibe Creation*: Freelance design, editing, and client interaction.
    * *Hadley Group IT Placement*: IT systems, troubleshooting, network support.
    * *Radisson Blu Internship*: Customer service, operations support.
  * *Education*: Focus on academic progression (Goa &rarr; Technology studies &rarr; London &rarr; West Thames College &rarr; University next).
  * *Leadership*: Focus on organization and initiative (Tiatr theater operations, Esports community tournaments).
  * *Awards*: Recognized excellence with context (Student of the Year, Orlim slow cycling win).
  * *Certifications*: Structured by themes (Networking, Digital Safety, Professional Development, Industry Experiences).

### 2.6 Contact Page
* **Core Question**: How can we work together?
* **Purpose**: Frictionless pathway for starting projects, university outreach, or direct email communication.
